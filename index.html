TextRunner

Personalized one-by-one messaging from your iPhone
● Runs from your Home Screen
On iPhone: open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It will launch like an app with its own icon.

Message template

Use {FirstName} and {FullName}.

Delay & batch

Batch starts at 1

Contacts

iPhone web apps cannot silently read your entire Contacts database. Use Import Contacts if your Safari/iOS version supports it, or add contacts manually. Each message still opens separately in Messages.

Controls

iOS will not let a web app press Send for you. TextRunner opens a separate pre-filled message for each selected person. You tap Send, then return to TextRunner and continue to the next contact.
